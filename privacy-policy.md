- Nothing is uploaded, synced, backed up, or sent anywhere.
- The app does not require or create any account, login, or profile.
- The app works entirely offline and has no server of its own.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind. No data about you, your device, or your usage is shared with any third party.

## Children's Privacy
